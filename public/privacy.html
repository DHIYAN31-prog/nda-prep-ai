NDA Prep AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

NDA Prep AI is an independent educational preparation tool. We collect account information needed for sign-in and store study data such as quiz attempts, bookmarks, plans and progress when you use those features.

Files

Files you upload to private study features are stored to provide the requested study functionality. Do not upload sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

The app may load content from services such as YouTube, news publishers and PDF.js. Optional AI features may use a configured AI provider. External services have their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact shown on the Google Play listing.
